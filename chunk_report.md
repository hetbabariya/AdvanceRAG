## 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Mistake #1: Buying Tools Before Having Clients
**Why it's bad:** Wastes money, creates pressure, makes you desperate
**Do instead:** Use free alternatives until you have revenue

### Mistake #2: Discounting Too Much
**Why it's bad:** Devalues your service, attracts cheap clients, unsustainable
**Do instead:** 50% off for FIRST 3-5 clients only, then regular pricing

### Mistake #3: Not Following Up
**Why it's bad:** Most clients need 3-5 touchpoints before buying
**Do instead:** Follow up every 3-5 days for 2 weeks

### Mistake #4: Trying to Be Perfect
**Why it's bad:** Delays progress, waste time, prevents learning
**Do instead:** Ship "good enough" and improve based on feedback

### Mistake #5: Not Asking for Payment
**Why it's bad:** No money = no business
**Do instead:** Always ask for commitment: "Can you do the advance today?"

### Mistake #6: Giving Up Too Soon
**Why it's bad:** Most businesses fail because owner quit, not because idea was bad
**Do instead:** Commit to 100 "NOs" before giving up
